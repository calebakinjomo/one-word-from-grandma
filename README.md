Exactly one word my grandma would text about AI.

## The word

**Terminator**

Grandma hears "AI" and her mind goes straight to the Terminator — warm, a little baffled, and suspicious of the machines all at once. It's the one word that is instantly recognizable as a grandma text about AI.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only) writes the word directly to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
